Note Pin for Obsidian puts your Obsidian notes in Android's notification bar and makes their reminders go off on time. It works with Note Pin, a free plugin you install in your Obsidian vault.

PIN NOTES TO THE NOTIFICATION BAR
A pinned note shows its title and first lines, tinted with the note's color, until you unpin it. Keep a shopping list, today's plan or a packing list one swipe away. Tap the notification and the note opens in Obsidian.

REMINDERS THAT GO OFF ON TIME
• At a set time, or all day: an all-day reminder stays in the notification bar until the day is over
• One-off, or repeating: every day, every week or every 2 weeks, every month on a date or a weekday (like the third Sunday), or every year, also by the Chinese lunar calendar. Repeats can stop on an end date
• Exact alarms, so they go off at the minute you set, even when Obsidian is closed
• After the phone restarts, the app sets your alarms and pins again

WORKS OFFLINE, WITH NO EXTERNAL SERVICES
• No account, no server, no ads and no analytics
• Obsidian hands your reminders to the app directly on your phone
• Your reminders are stored in your notes, so they sync with whatever you already use

HOW IT WORKS
1. In Obsidian, install the Note Pin plugin from Settings → Community plugins.
2. Right-click a note, choose Set reminder, and pick a time, or pin the note to the status bar.
3. Open Obsidian on your phone. The first time, this app asks whether to allow your vault.

Note Pin for Obsidian is an unofficial companion app. It isn't made by or affiliated with the Obsidian team.
